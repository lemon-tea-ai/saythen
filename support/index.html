Support for SayThen: Voice to Tasks.

SayThen is preparing for its first App Store release. Hosted interpretation is operating; Pro purchase and iCloud features are still undergoing release verification.

Contact

Email support@ramblepractice.com with your app and iOS versions and a reproducible example. Do not send credentials or private recordings and transcripts unnecessarily.

Capture and review

Speak or type a thought, then review the proposed actions, especially dates and times, before confirming them. Today shows current and upcoming items; Memory helps you find previous thoughts. Voice recordings are limited to five minutes.

Connection and processing

Interpretation requires an internet connection. SayThen sends transcript text to its hosted service, which uses OpenAI and Jev/Typesafe. There is no custom-server setup or switch to a local-only interpreter in the current production workflow. Original audio is not sent to these AI providers by SayThen. See our privacy policy for Apple speech recognition, provider retention, and other data handling.

If processing fails or the service asks you to retry, keep your draft and try again later. Failed processing does not count toward the monthly allowance. Check microphone and speech-recognition permissions in iPhone Settings if voice capture fails; you can type instead.

Free and Pro launch plans

Free includes 30 successful rambles each month, local storage, and the past seven days of Memory plus future and unresolved items. Older resolved history is hidden, not erased. Export and deletion remain available.

SayThen Pro is planned at US$5.99/month or US$49.99/year, with local storefront prices shown by Apple before purchase. Both plans include unlimited rambles subject to fair use, full Memory, and optional iCloud sync and restore. Purchase availability depends on the released app version.

Fair use

Pro is intended for normal personal use. Automated or bulk processing, resale, and sharing service credentials are not supported. Five-minute recording limits and processing-rate controls apply. Unusual bursts may be temporarily throttled; retry after the displayed interval. These protections do not delete saved items. There is no advertised monthly Pro capture ceiling.

Sync and restoring a new iPhone

When released, Pro iCloud sync must be enabled in Settings and finish uploading before it can restore history elsewhere. Use the same iCloud account and an active Pro subscription on your new iPhone. Original recordings stay on their original device. Apple Calendar and Reminders sync their own items separately. Cloud sync is not a versioned backup.

Turning sync off or allowing Pro to expire pauses synchronization; it does not erase saved history. Restoring purchases restores subscription access, not local-only history that was never uploaded.

Export and deletion

Settings → Export my data exports retained local history. Remove from this device removes local captures and recordings. Delete everywhere, when available, deletes synced SayThen history from the current iCloud account once it completes online. Neither operation deletes Calendar/Reminders items or your separate exports. See the privacy policy for credentials, server security records, and provider data that are handled separately.

Manage subscriptions

Use Restore purchases in the app’s plan screen if an eligible purchase is missing. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Purchases use Apple’s standard license agreement.

Calendar and Reminders

Allow permissions when prompted and confirm the details before saving. If a previously synced action reports a recovery or duplicate-prevention issue, check the destination app and original device before retrying; avoid manually recreating the same item repeatedly.
