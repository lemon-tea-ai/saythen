Support for SayThen: Voice to Tasks.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email support@ramblepractice.com. Please do not send recordings, transcripts, access tokens, payment card details, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

Get started

Open SayThen and use the microphone to record a thought, or use keyboard capture to type one. Review the proposed actions, especially dates and times, before confirming them. Use Today for current and upcoming items and Memory to search saved thoughts.

Microphone or speech recognition isn’t working

Check SayThen’s permissions in iPhone Settings. Speech recognition uses on-device processing when supported; otherwise Apple’s recognition service may require a connection. You can use typed capture instead. A failed or interrupted recording may be kept locally for retry.

Calendar and Reminders

Allow the relevant permissions when prompted. Calendar events need clear start and end times. Check ambiguous times before saving. Items created in Apple Calendar or Reminders may sync according to your settings in those apps.

Language support

The current local interpreter supports common English instructions. Review its results; it may not understand every expression or ambiguous date.

Export or delete your history

Open Settings → Privacy → Export my data or Delete my local data. Deleting local history does not delete items already created in Apple Calendar or Reminders. Undo those actions first, or remove them in the destination app. Copies you previously exported must be deleted separately.

Original recordings

Settings → Keep original audio controls retention after successful processing. It is off by default. Pending recordings remain until processed or deleted.

Connected interpretation

If you configure and enable a service in Settings, transcripts and language/time context are sent to that service. Check its operator and privacy practices before connecting. Turn off Use connected interpretation to return to local interpretation.

Accounts and sync

The current core experience requires no account. Cross-device account sync is not currently available.

Purchases

Hosted AI and premium plans are planned for launch and are not currently available. These pages will be updated with the final service and purchase details before launch. If you have an eligible App Store purchase in a later version, use Restore purchases in the plan screen. Manage or cancel Apple subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting an app does not cancel a subscription.

Reporting a problem

Include your iOS version, SayThen version, and steps to reproduce the issue using a made-up example. Please do not post private thoughts, audio, calendar details, or credentials.
