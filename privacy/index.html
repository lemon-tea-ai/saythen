Privacy policy

Your thoughts, handled with care.

Effective September 20, 2026 · Current local-first version

This policy describes SayThen: Voice to Tasks, provided by Ke Zhu, and this support website. It covers the current app, including optional connected interpretation when you configure it.

Information stored on your device

SayThen stores your captures, transcripts, suggested actions, notes, dates, processing status, and links to created Calendar or Reminders items on your device. A randomly generated local identity and any service access token you configure are kept in the iOS Keychain. App preferences are kept locally. The current app does not require an online account and does not offer cloud account sync.

Audio and Apple speech recognition

Voice capture requires microphone permission. SayThen uses Apple’s Speech framework and requests on-device recognition when your device and language support it. Otherwise, audio may be processed by Apple’s speech recognition service. See Apple’s privacy information for Apple’s practices.

Original audio is deleted after successful processing by default. If you enable Keep original audio, it remains locally. Failed or pending recordings stay locally for retry until processed or deleted.

Optional connected interpretation

Local interpretation does not send your transcript to a SayThen interpretation server. If you choose to configure and enable a service, the app sends your transcript, current time, locale, and timezone to the address you specify, with the configured authorization token. Network communication also exposes connection information such as your IP address to that service.

The compatible reference service uses OpenAI for extraction and may use Typesafe/Jev for classification. Those providers receive text needed to process the request. Service operators and providers may have their own retention and processing practices. Do not connect to a service unless you trust its operator and understand those practices. Disabling connected interpretation stops future interpretation requests but does not delete data already received by a service.

Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, SayThen creates and manages the actions you choose in Apple Calendar or Reminders. Those apps and their configured accounts control any subsequent synchronization. SayThen does not upload your calendar or reminders database to this website.

Diagnostics and tracking

The current app keeps event counters and processing measurements in memory on your device. They do not contain your transcript or audio and are not sent to an analytics service. The app does not include advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Purchases

If App Store purchases are available in your version, Apple handles payment information. SayThen uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement information to determine access. SayThen does not receive your payment card details. Hosted AI and premium plans are planned for launch and are not currently available. These pages will be updated with the final service and purchase details before launch.

Retention, export, and deletion

Saved thoughts remain locally until you delete them. Settings includes data export and local deletion. Local deletion removes capture history and recordings, but does not remove existing Calendar or Reminders items, separately shared exports, or data already received by an optional service. Keychain identity, connection credentials, and app preferences are separate from capture history; deleting local history is not a complete reset of all settings. Device backups may retain copies according to your Apple or computer backup settings.

Support and this website

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide and use it to respond to your request. This site has no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts it; GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Information posted in GitHub issues is public.

Your choices

You can type instead of recording, revoke microphone, speech recognition, Calendar, or Reminders permissions in iOS Settings, disable connected interpretation, choose whether to keep audio, and export or delete your local captures.

Changes and questions

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. A future hosted service, account system, or new data use will need an updated policy.

For support or privacy questions, email support@ramblepractice.com. Please do not send recordings, transcripts, access tokens, payment card details, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.
