Privacy policy

Your thoughts, handled with care.

Updated September 21, 2026 · Hosted interpretation service

This policy describes SayThen: Voice to Tasks, provided by Ke Zhu, its hosted interpretation service, and this website. SayThen is preparing for its first App Store release. The hosted service is operating; Pro purchases and iCloud sync remain subject to release verification. The sections below explain how those features handle data when available and enabled.

What stays on your device

SayThen stores captures, transcripts, suggested actions, notes, dates, completion and processing state, and references to items created in Calendar or Reminders on your device. Preferences and monthly usage records are stored locally. An automatically issued installation credential is kept in the iOS Keychain. Basic use does not require creating a SayThen account.

Voice recordings and Apple speech recognition

Voice capture requires microphone and speech-recognition permissions. Apple’s Speech framework uses on-device recognition where supported; otherwise Apple may process audio to transcribe it. See Apple’s privacy information. Typing avoids audio recording but still uses hosted text interpretation.

Audio is not uploaded to SayThen’s interpretation backend, OpenAI, Jev/Typesafe, or SayThen’s iCloud sync. Recordings are deleted after successful interpretation by default. If you enable Keep original audio, recordings remain on the original device. Failed or pending recordings may remain for retry until processed or deleted. Device backups are controlled separately by your Apple or computer backup settings.

Hosted AI interpretation

When you submit a voice transcript or typed thought, SayThen sends its text, current time, timezone, and language/locale to our HTTPS service hosted on Railway in the United States. The service sends text and time/language context to OpenAI to extract suggested actions. Jev, provided by Typesafe AI, receives the transcript and suggested action details to classify actions. Text you include about other people or sensitive subjects is part of the submitted content. These providers do not receive your original recording through this workflow.

Hosted interpretation is part of the current app workflow and requires a connection. It is not an optional server that you configure yourself. If you do not want a thought sent to these services, do not submit it for interpretation.

Service providers and retention

The SayThen application server processes transcripts and results in memory and does not intentionally write them to its database or application logs. This does not mean providers retain no data.

  • OpenAI: We disable response storage in interpretation requests. OpenAI’s default API policy may still retain prompts, responses, and related metadata in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, with longer retention possible for legal or safety reasons. OpenAI states that API data is not used for model training by default unless the customer opts in. We do not claim a verified zero-data-retention arrangement. See OpenAI’s API data controls.
  • Typesafe AI / Jev: Its policy covers API inputs and states that inputs are not used to train or fine-tune its models. Its published policy does not give one fixed deletion deadline for all API inputs. We do not promise immediate deletion or zero retention by Typesafe. See Typesafe’s privacy policy.
  • Railway: Railway hosts the service and receives network and operational information. It captures application logs; retention depends on the hosting plan and applicable provider practices. See Railway’s privacy policy and log documentation.

Installation identifiers, security, and diagnostics

To authenticate installations and prevent abuse, our server stores a hash of each installation credential and its creation time. These records currently have no automatic expiry. Do not send your credential to support. Registration protection stores a hashed network address with a date and daily registration count; previous-day entries are removed when the next registration is processed. Request-rate counters use installation and network-address information temporarily in server memory.

Operational logs contain processing times, action and review counts, and failure counts. The configured application logger does not include transcripts, action text, recordings, or credentials in those metrics. Hosting infrastructure also handles connection information such as IP addresses. On-device event counters are separate from these server logs. The app contains no advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking.

Optional Pro iCloud sync and restore

When available, Pro sync is opt-in and uses Apple CloudKit’s private database for your iCloud account. It synchronizes transcripts, actions, edits, completion state, record identifiers, conflict-resolution metadata, and deletion markers. Device identifiers and routing claims help avoid duplicate external actions. Recordings, service credentials, local usage counters, and local Calendar/Reminders object identifiers are not included in those sync payloads.

Restore on a new iPhone requires an active Pro subscription, the same iCloud account, and successful prior uploads. Sync is not a versioned backup. Turning sync off or letting Pro expire pauses sync; it does not itself erase previously saved local or cloud history. Changing iCloud accounts separates the previous account’s local history to avoid uploading it to another account.

Calendar, Reminders, and purchases

With permission, SayThen creates and manages the Calendar or Reminders items you confirm. Apple and your configured accounts control their subsequent sync. SayThen does not upload your entire Calendar or Reminders database to its AI service.

Apple processes subscription payments. SayThen uses StoreKit product, transaction, and entitlement information to determine Pro access. We do not receive your payment-card details. Restoring a subscription restores access; it does not recover history that was never synced.

Your choices, export, and deletion

  • Use Settings → Export my data to export retained local history, including history hidden by the Free Memory window.
  • Remove from this device deletes local capture history and recordings. It does not delete prior iCloud copies, security records on our server, Keychain credentials, separate exports, or Calendar/Reminders items.
  • When iCloud sync is available, Delete everywhere requests deletion of SayThen history from the current iCloud account. It needs a connection and successful completion; other devices receive deletion when they sync. Deletion markers and routing metadata may remain to prevent stale records or duplicate external actions.
  • Revoke microphone, speech recognition, Calendar, or Reminders permissions in iOS Settings; choose whether to keep original recordings; or turn off iCloud sync.

The Free seven-day Memory window restricts access to older resolved items; it does not automatically delete them. Export and deletion remain available without Pro. Removing the app or local history does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase data already processed by service providers. Backups and exported copies must be managed separately.

For access, correction, or deletion requests involving our hosted service or support correspondence, contact support@ramblepractice.com. We may need limited information to locate and verify the relevant records, and will explain what we can delete and what must remain for security or legal reasons. Do not email passwords, installation tokens, or API keys.

Support, website, and international processing

If you contact us, we use the details you supply to respond and keep a record of the request; deleting app history does not erase correspondence. This website has no advertising pixels, analytics scripts, or contact form. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Anything you post in a public GitHub issue is public.

Our backend is hosted in the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries under their applicable policies and agreements. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or request deletion of personal information, or complain to your local privacy authority. Contact us to exercise those rights.

Children and policy changes

SayThen is a general productivity service and is not designed specifically for children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and address it. An App Store content rating is not a statement that every hosted provider permits use by children.

We will update this page when our features, providers, or data practices change. For privacy questions, email support@ramblepractice.com.
